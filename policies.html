RENTAL POLICIES

Please review all seven official rental policies for L&D Trailers before taking possession of any equipment.

1. PAYMENT POLICY

Rental fees and security deposits must be paid before the trailer leaves our possession.

2. SECURITY DEPOSIT POLICY

Security deposits are refundable after the trailer is returned and inspected. Deductions may apply for documented damage, missing equipment, unpaid charges, or violations of the rental agreement.

3. LATE RETURN POLICY

Customers must return trailers at the agreed-upon time. Late returns require prior approval. Proposed late fee: $15 per hour, up to the applicable additional daily rental charge. Any additional charges must be disclosed in the rental agreement.

4. CANCELLATION POLICY

Customers should provide at least 24 hours' notice when canceling a reservation. Cancellation and refund terms should be displayed before payment.

5. DAMAGE POLICY

Customers are responsible for damage occurring during their rental period, subject to the signed rental agreement and applicable law. All trailers must be inspected and photographed before pickup and after return.

Inspection Protocol: High-resolution photos are taken at hookup and return to guarantee fair, documented deposit refunds.

6. CLEANING POLICY

Trailers must be returned reasonably clean and free of trash and debris. Additional cleaning charges may apply if a trailer requires cleaning beyond normal use.

7. PROHIBITED USE

Customers may not:

  • Exceed the trailer's rated weight capacity.
  • Transport prohibited or illegal materials.
  • Use trailers for unauthorized commercial purposes.
  • Modify or alter trailers.
  • Allow unauthorized individuals to use the rental.
  • Tow trailers with an unsafe or improperly equipped vehicle.
Safe Towing Requirement: Proper hitch ball, crossed safety chains, and functioning electrical harness required before departure.

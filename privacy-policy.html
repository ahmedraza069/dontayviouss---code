PRIVACY POLICY

DATA SECURITY & PRIVACY NOTICE

At L&D Trailers in Corinth, Mississippi, your privacy is paramount. This policy outlines how we handle customer contact details, reservation requests, and payment processing.

Information We Collect

When submitting an online reservation or inquiry, we collect your full name, phone number, email address, intended use of the trailer, and driver's license details for identification and verification at pickup.

Online Payment Security

We integrate with trusted payment processors such as Stripe and Wix Payments. Customer credit card information is processed using bank-level 256-bit SSL encryption and is never stored directly on our website servers or database.

Contact Regarding Your Data

If you have questions regarding your rental records, please contact us at rentals@ldtrailers.com or call (662) 555-0199.

L&D TRAILERS RENTAL TERMS

By submitting a booking request and taking possession of a utility trailer from L&D Trailers in Corinth, Mississippi, the customer agrees to adhere to all terms, policies, and conditions outlined in the rental agreement and website disclosures.

1. Approval & Possession

Submitting a booking request does not guarantee equipment availability. All booking requests are reviewed and approved by L&D Trailers before confirmation. Customers must present a valid driver's license, proof of insurance, and sign the written rental agreement prior to taking possession.

2. Payment & Security Deposits

Rental fees and refundable security deposits must be paid before the trailer leaves our possession. The security deposit is separate from the rental charge and is refunded after the trailer is returned and inspected, provided there are no outstanding charges or documented damage.

3. Late Returns & Overtime

Customers must return trailers at the agreed-upon time. Late returns require prior approval. Late fees are assessed at $15 per hour, up to the applicable additional daily rental charge.
